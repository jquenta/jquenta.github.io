# About Me

<div class="about-container"> 
  <div class="about-text" markdown="1">
    
  Hi! I'm **Johann Quenta Raygada**, a PhD Student in Theoretical Particle Physics at [SISSA](https://www.sissa.it/tpp/), supervised by Pavel Putrov at [ICTP](https://www.ictp.it/hecap). I'm originally from Peru, where I did my Bachelor's degree in Physics at the [Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú](https://www.pucp.edu.pe/carrera/fisica/). After that, I did the Diploma Programme in High Energy, Cosmology and Astroparticle Physics at ICTP, and the M2 year of the Math4Phys Master's degree in Mathematical Physics at [Université Bourgogne Europe](https://math.ube.fr/math4phys).

  My current research lies in the broad area of **topological aspects of quantum field theory**, and approaches it from both a physical and mathematical perspective. This includes topics like generalized symmetries, topological quantum field theories, quantum invariants of links and manifolds, topological phases of matter, and more.
