# Research

## Approximating $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Chern-Simons theory by finite group gauge theories

<div class="research-item">
  
  <div class="research-text" markdown="1">

  In [this]() paper, we argue that (2+1)d twisted Dijkgraaf-Witten (DW) theories with gauge group $\mathrm{SL}(2,q)$ encode many of the same topological invariants as the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Chern-Simons (CS) theory at large levels. Namely, the former know about the values of the CS invariant on flat $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ connections, as well as their associated leading powers in $k$ in the asymptotic expansion of the CS partition function. Our claims are supported by explicit computations of the DW partition function on a variety of (non-hyperbolic) 3-manifolds, which are possible after determining the modular data of the discrete gauge theories. We also provide some ideas for a proof when the 3-manifold is spherical, but otherwise, the mathematics underlying these relations remain a mystery!

  *Joint work with Pavel Putrov and Thomas Nicosanti.*
